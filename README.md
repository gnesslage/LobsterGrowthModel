# LobsterGrowthModel
This is a two-stage crustacean growth model that estimates molt probability and growth increment simultaneously using mark-recapture data. This model uses mark-recapture data to provide annual estimates of growth. A seasonal version is also available upon request (contact Genny Nesslage, nesslage@umces.edu). The model corrects for two potential sources of bias in the use of tagging data: 1) differential length- and sex-based recapture and reporting selectivity (i.e., sex- and size-based probability of receiving a recapture report), and 2) differential tag retention rates between animals that grow and those that do not. The growth model can also incorporate the effect of temperature on growth probability. Estimated growth parameters can used to generate sex-specific, seasonal growth transition matrices and simulate growth in the absence of fishing mortality.

This model was developed in support of the 2025 ASMFC American lobster stock assessment with funding provided by National Sea Grant American Lobster Research Program 2023 (Award NA23OAR4170577).
